We value and celebrate the memories of people: therefore, the user experience is the forefront of this cause.

We have developed algorithms to make the preprocessing and recreation of an individual's likeness from local or online data a seamless experience.

We have developed the ability to use natural language to update an avatar's identity to recreate the likeness of those without access to a plethora of data.

We value our users and will work with them to make certain the avatar's likeness celebrates the existence of that individual's life.
